# Interface direction

The property operator needs a quiet, efficient workspace. Guests need to understand the hotel and reserve a stay confidently. The visual identity takes its cue from shaded coastal verandas and the considered hospitality of a small independent property.

Tokens: deep tidal ink #173C43, sea glass #E5EFED, cool paper #F6F8F7, white #FFFFFF, muted teal #697B7D, brass focus accent #A88045. Libre Caslon Display appears in welcome/page titles; DM Sans handles forms, tables and operational data. Spacious content areas sit beside a compact fixed navigation rail. The distinctive gesture is an architectural arch on the account screens, kept away from operational forms.

Public direction: photography-led hotel storytelling with a compact persistent booking path; owner-controlled design tokens and sections. Avoid generic dashboard cards in the public hero. Brand defaults are editable presentation values, not hotel facts. Empty operational tables invite setup; they never fabricate activity or revenue.

Responsive rules: stacked account screens, wrapped mobile navigation, two-column compact metrics, horizontally scrollable data tables, single-column editing forms. Form labels, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image text and reduced motion support are required. Browser visual inspection remains a separate acceptance gate.
